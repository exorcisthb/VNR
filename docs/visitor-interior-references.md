# Visitor interior reference notes

Updated 2026-11-02. These are compact visitor reconstructions. Published photographs
support the room types and visible furniture; room coordinates and dimensions in
this game are not surveyed floor plans.

## Independence Palace

Official museum gallery, including present-day photographs of the Cabinet Room
and presidential office:
https://dinhdoclap.gov.vn/anh-dinh-doc-lap-noi-luu-giu-dau-an-sau-50-nam-giai-phong/

The presidential office now has a wooden panelled desk, red upholstered main
chair, three telephones, low wood panelling, landscape painting surround and a
separate guest seating area. The painting is a generic procedural landscape,
not a reproduction of the original artwork. This room occupies the formerly
sparse east wing beside the Credentials Room in the visitor map. That placement
is a game layout choice, not a claim about the original room's exact coordinates.
Twenty Cabinet Room chairs follow the oval table, facing inward.

## Government buildings

The former generic open office is replaced with documented public room functions:

- Reception room: light upholstered wooden seats, two principal seats, opposing
  guest rows, tea tables and landscape backdrop. Reference:
  https://baochinhphu.vn/thu-tuong-pham-minh-chinh-tiep-bo-truong-quoc-phong-nhat-ban-102240806182511406.htm
- Video conference room: table, microphones and screens. Reference:
  https://baochinhphu.vn/ngay-nay-10-nam-truoc-hoi-nghi-truyen-hinh-truc-tuyen-dau-tien-cua-chinh-phu-voi-63-dia-phuong-102253993.htm
- Information and operations centre: public inauguration on 19 August 2020;
  display wall and operator consoles represent its published function. Reference:
  https://media.chinhphu.vn/khai-truong-trung-tam-thong-tin-chi-dao-dieu-hanh-va-he-thong-thong-tin-bao-cao-quoc-gia-102277748.htm

The screens contain explicitly marked illustrative diagrams. The rooms represent
public functions of the Government's premises and systems, not a reconstruction
of the exact layout of the building at 1 Hoang Hoa Tham. Public sources inspected
do not establish the furniture layout of private staff offices, so none is
presented as an exact replica.

## Safety and signage

Room signs use two front-facing planes with opposite orientations, so neither
side mirrors the lettering. Stairwell decks have rails and collision barriers
along both long edges and the lower-end opening, with short returns and a clear
passage at the upper landing. Exterior entrance stair flights also have side rails.
Physics tests cover the stairwell barriers and new room spawn clearances.
